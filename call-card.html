Ask these

  • "What are you paying Angi or Thumbtack right now?" — the sore spot. Let him vent.
  • "How many of those leads actually turn into jobs?"
  • "Do you have a website right now?"
  • "When someone calls you, are you picking up or is it going to voicemail?"
  • "Whose name is the domain in?" (if he has one)

Say these

Opening "I build websites for gutter companies. Yours is already built — I can text you the link right now, take a look and tell me if it's wrong."

On price "Five hundred, one time. No monthly. One job pays for it four times over."

On Angi "A lead off your own website is yours alone. Nobody else got it."

On why so cheap "It's one page, not a twenty-page thing, and I've got the template. Takes me hours, not weeks. I'd rather do a lot of them than charge one guy six grand."

Closing "I'll text you a link to pay. Once it's paid I'll point it at your domain today."

Cash / check / VenmoNo. Never to your own account. Everything through the link.

Never say

  • "It's done" — until Tim confirms the link is live. Text him first, takes ten seconds. This already bit us once.
  • "Licensed and insured" about any client, unless he told you so himself.
  • "We'll get you on page one of Google." Nobody can promise that.
  • "We'll call your leads for you." An AI making those calls is illegal. We don't sell it.
  • Any monthly service. Doesn't exist yet. Don't presell it.

If he stalls

  • "I need to think about it." → "Sure. The site's live at that link either way, take a look tonight. I'll check back Thursday."
  • "I already have a website." → "Pull it up on your phone right now. Does it look right?" Most don't.
  • "My nephew is doing one." → "How long's that been in the works?" Usually months. Ours is done today.
  • "Too expensive." → "What do you spend on Angi in a month?" It's almost always more.
