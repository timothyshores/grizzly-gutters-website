Hosting and domains, in plain English

Right now every site lives on a free GitHub address that looks like timothyshores.github.io/their-business-name. It's real, it's live, it loads fast, it works on phones. Fine for the demo and fine for a client who doesn't care.

When a client wants his own name — saltlakegutterguys.com — Tim points it at the same site. Nothing gets rebuilt, the link just changes. Takes a day, mostly waiting.

Who buys the domain matters. Have the client buy it in his own name and put it on his own card. It's $12–15/year, he'll say yes, and it means he owns his own name. It also means we never eat a renewal for a client who stops answering.

If he already has a domain pointed at some old dead site, that's fine and actually easier. Get his login for wherever he bought it and Tim redirects it.

One honest caveat, Tim's problem not yours: GitHub's free hosting is aimed at projects, not businesses. Nobody enforces this against a small local brochure site, and we're nowhere near any limit. But once real client money is involved, Tim will likely move these to proper hosting — still a few dollars a month, still invisible to the client. Don't bring it up on a call.

Questions for Jason

  1. Where should new leads go? Email or cell. The contact form needs a destination. Right now it routes to us.
  2. Any job photos on your phone? Even bad ones. Site photos are stock. Real ones are the fastest visible upgrade and cost nothing.
  3. What address do you want listed? Directories disagree. Need his answer before we publish one.
  4. Is your license current, and do you want "Licensed and Insured" on the site? Ask exactly that way. Public records show expired 11/30/2023. We will not put that claim up until he confirms, because a false one is his liability.
  5. Do you carry general liability insurance? Which carrier? Homeowners ask. If he has it, we display it.
  6. Are you bonded? Workers comp? Licensed plus bonded plus insured is the standard trust cluster competitors show.
  7. How many years have you been doing this? Every competitor leads with this. His site currently says nothing.
  8. How far out do you travel? Sets the service area copy.
  9. Do you have a Google Business Profile? This is the follow-up sale and how his review count actually grows.
